# Standard Clarification Language (starting points — adapt per project)

These are drafts based on JMS positions. Management wanted formal standard RFI and clarification verbiage written at project start, so check whether an official version exists before using these. Mark any text used as "draft" until the PE confirms it.

## Screw-conveyor slide / wedge gates — pressure rating and leakage

Based on Bio-HANDLING engineering policy (Jul 2026):

> JMS screw conveyor slide/wedge gates are custom isolation gates designed for dewatered biosolids service. They are not AWWA C561 sluice gates and are not designed or tested to [40 05 59.35 / sluice or slide gate specification] beyond the material requirements. No leakage rate is guaranteed. Where the specification does not state a pressure rating, gates will be shop tested to a baseline of [2–3] psi. Gates are designed for fully open or fully closed operation. Discharge rate is controlled by screw speed, not by partial gate opening.

Notes:
- Some projects carried 10 psi. Use the project value if the spec states one and JMS agreed to it.
- No gate manufacturer recommends horizontal installation. Note this if the layout requires it.

## Seismic — SDC B

> Per ASCE 7 Chapter 13, nonstructural components in Seismic Design Category B are exempt from seismic design requirements. Seismic calculations are not provided for this equipment.

## Fully stainless equipment — coatings

> Equipment is fabricated entirely of stainless steel and receives no coating system. A passivation procedure covering cross-contamination control and weld heat-tint removal is provided in lieu of 09 96 00 coating submittals.
